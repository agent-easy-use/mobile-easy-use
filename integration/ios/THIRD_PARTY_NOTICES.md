# Third-party native component

The `Binaries` directories contain renamed official Frida Gadget binaries: `17.16.4` for physical
iOS devices and `17.9.11` for the iOS Simulator. The adjacent `MobileEasyUse.dylib` binaries are
built from this repository's Objective-C bridge sources and directly load those Gadget runtimes.
Frida source, releases, and license information are available from
<https://github.com/frida/frida>.
